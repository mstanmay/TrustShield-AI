# SEBI TechSprint — Winning Pitch Summary

## Problem Statement
Indian retail investors lose hundreds of crores annually to sophisticated scams:
1. Deepfake videos of prominent financial advisors / SEBI officials on YouTube & Instagram.
2. Voice-cloned robocalls impersonating SEBI investigation officers.
3. Forged SEBI circulars, letters, and fake registration certificates.
4. Typosquatted phishing domains (`sebl.gov.in`) stealing Demat credentials.
5. WhatsApp / Telegram pump-and-dump groups guaranteeing 100% stock returns.

## Our Solution: The SEBI AI Trust Platform
An enterprise-grade, multi-modal, real-time AI platform protecting retail investors at every touchpoint:
- **Multi-Modal AI Engine**: Video (deepfake), Audio (voice cloning), Document (OCR forgery), URL (phishing), QR code & Messaging analysis.
- **RAG Knowledge Base**: Indexed regulatory corpus (SEBI, NSE, BSE, RBI, CERT-In) providing instant policy verification.
- **Neo4j Fraud Graph**: Discovers organized scam syndicates connecting phone numbers, Telegram groups, and crypto wallets.
- **Real-Time Browser Extension**: Sub-100ms URL & DOM protection warning investors *before* they enter credentials.
- **Auto SCORES Complaint Assistant**: Generates structured SEBI SCORES complaint drafts ready for filing.
- **Production MLOps & Observability**: Complete MLflow model tracking, Prometheus metrics, Grafana dashboards, Jaeger distributed tracing, and Loki centralized logging.
